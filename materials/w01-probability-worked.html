Frequency and repeated observations

Does an agreement fraction of 4/64/6 establish a long-run agreement rate for future tickets?

No. A frequency account connects probability to relative frequency in a specified repeatable process. If IiI_i indicates agreement on trial ii, the observed frequency after nn trials is n−1∑i=1nIin^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n I_i; a limiting account relates this sequence to a probability pp. Six observations alone establish no such limit and do not establish that future tickets follow the same process.

Keep observed frequency, model probability and future estimation distinct.

Reference: course 01.1 notes, §§1–8; rigorous supplement for formal derivations.

Belief and evidence

Suppose an analyst assigns probability 0.8 to agreement on the next ticket, given this audit and other information. Does the table alone determine 0.8?

No. A subjective account treats probability as a degree of belief given information. The assignment needs stated information or judgments and must be coherent with the analyst’s other probability assignments. An evidential account also needs a specified rule connecting evidence and background assumptions to a number.

State that these are interpretations, not competing arithmetic rules for the same fully specified experiment.

Reference: course 01.1 notes, §§1–8; rigorous supplement for formal derivations.

What the earlier views contribute

What does each account emphasize: equal-likelihood counting, frequency, belief, or evidential support? What remains necessary for a calculation?

The accounts emphasize symmetry, repeated observations, information-conditioned belief, and evidential support. A calculation still needs specified outcomes, an event and a probability assignment. A propensity interpretation instead locates chance in a specified physical or randomized mechanism.

Use the slide as a transition, not a verdict on philosophical interpretations.
