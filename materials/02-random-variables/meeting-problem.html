Two friends: the same event under two measures

Both friends arrive independently within one hour. The first waits for the selected allowance, even beyond the end of that hour. The meeting event is |TA − TB| ≤ δ.

Both arrival models are independent. Changing the waiting allowance changes the event. Comparing panels at the same allowance changes only the measure. The square and its event regions remain the same.

Probabilities use the exact formulas, not a simulation or a grid estimate. For δ = minutes/60, no meeting has probability (1−δ)² under uniform arrivals and (1−δ)³(1+δ/3) under decreasing-density arrivals. The density tiles are only a visual approximation.
