Teaching with the live deck

Use Pen to write and Navigate to click links. Arrow keys change slides. Ink remains while you move through the deck. Save ink downloads your annotations; Load ink restores them. Save before refreshing or closing the tab. Alternatively, open the writing PDF in your usual annotation app.

The main decks have 35 matching content slides, plus the title slide. They use a six-ticket routing audit to introduce interpretations of probability, sets and events, the probability triplet, its axioms, a random variable, and continuous ticket-time calculations. The pace is gradual and may continue across meetings. Formal derivations are in the separate supplement.

Experiments

Sampling maps, inverses and chord length: examine each parametrization and the corresponding event in the uniform source.

  • Bertrand’s chords: compare three sampling mechanisms.
  • Outcomes, events and weights: switch between fair and loaded dice, and between full and parity-only event collections.
  • Running frequencies: compare observed fractions with a specified model probability.
