The worked student deck has 35 content slides, plus the title slide. It uses a six-ticket routing audit to introduce interpretations of probability, sets and events, the probability triplet, its axioms, a random variable, and continuous ticket-time calculations. The pace is gradual and may continue across meetings. Formal derivations are in the separate supplement.

Experiments

Sampling maps, inverses and chord length: examine each parametrization and the corresponding event in the uniform source.

  • Bertrand’s chords: compare three sampling mechanisms.
  • Outcomes, events and weights: switch between fair and loaded dice, and between full and parity-only event collections.
  • Running frequencies: compare observed fractions with a specified model probability.
